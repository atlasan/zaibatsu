---
title: Printed arrows decorate internal space links
type: decision
---
Use Block.spaceArrows from/to IDs over symmetric physical neighbors for internal one-way arrows. Only reverse traversal of that link is barred; other links and rotated edge exits remain available. Octagon bypasses arrow restrictions while attached. Legacy Space.direction is a separate cross-edge field and must not encode Hacktivism/Sprawl arrows. See SR-MOVE-007 and ADR-0016; full global block effects remain provisional.
