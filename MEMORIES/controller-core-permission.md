---
title: Optional Core permission is distinct from setup identity
type: decision
---
Clandestine Broadcast's typed under-control `controller-core` effect names its large unlimited special landing space. Derive optional Reboot destinations from current block ownership; do not mark it `isCentralCore`, change the pile/setup, grant Reboot or move pawns on capture/loss. Omitted Reboot destinations preserve original Core behavior. See [ADR-0020](../DOCS/adr/0020-controller-core-permission.md).
