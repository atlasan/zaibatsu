title: Native free Search has its own physical use marker
type: decision
---
Drone Probe has independent once-per-turn Search and five-step movement. Accepted Search commits its marker and reveals before authorized placement without a card payment; invalid placement preserves that accepted reveal/use. Public eligibility checks edges, occupancy and pile count without hidden-top inspection or RNG changes. Both native markers follow the physical control card through takeover/common release and clear only at its current controller beginning. Module-granted free Delete creates no paid badge. See [pawn rules](../DOCS/rules/speedrunners/pawns-abilities-and-cards.md), SR-PAWN-007, and [board rules](../DOCS/rules/speedrunners/board-and-movement.md), SR-BOARD-019. Current source protocol/checksum is v20/block-bonus-counters; full corpus/modes and separate Sprawl/EMP/used-card gaps stay open.
