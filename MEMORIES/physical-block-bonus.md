---
title: A block Bonus counter is a physical allocation with a collection receipt
type: decision
---
Arms Industry allocates one reserve piece on placement; control transfers that same piece once. PlacedBlock bonusCounter exists after placement, with collectedBy absent while held and retained as the first-recipient receipt after transfer. Keep it separate from Bonus icons and include both in pool.onBoard. Combined icon/block funding is checked before placement; collection works at empty reserve without minting another piece. Storage is not a movement space. See [ADR-0026](../DOCS/adr/0026-physical-bonus-counters-on-blocks.md), SR-BOARD-021 and SR-BONUS-002. T-RULES-031/047 still gates reserve exhaustion and strict official acceptance. That delivery used source protocol v20/block-bonus-counters; the later Cleaner B delivery advances the current protocol to v21. Frozen regression stays unchanged.
