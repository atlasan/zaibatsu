title: Global sacrifice selections belong to each affected controller
type: decision
---
Electromagnetic Pulse uses public per-owner `block-sacrifice` choices, including permitted off-turn responses. Snapshot/replay the batch, then revalidate every selected pawn before deferred normal elimination/refunds. Executable options and unresolved prevention targets are separate. Public-commit/seat-order timing and retained eligibility remain provisional T-RULES-029; strict official bundles reject them. See [ADR-0018](../DOCS/adr/0018-public-global-sacrifice-decisions.md).
