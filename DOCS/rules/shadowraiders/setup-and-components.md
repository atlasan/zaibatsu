# Shadowraiders rules — setup and components

## SH-SETUP-001 — Expansion relationship

- **Source:** `sh-en-rulebook` — pp. 2–3, “Introduction”.
- **Applies to:** Shadowraiders.
- **Maturity:** planned.
- **Rule:** Shadowraiders extends Speedrunners. Base-game rules apply unless a
  Shadowraiders rule explicitly replaces them.

## SH-SETUP-002 — Added components

- **Source:** `sh-en-rulebook` — pp. 2–3, “Components”.
- **Applies to:** Shadowraiders.
- **Maturity:** partial.
- **Rule:** The expansion adds its own blocks, action/control cards, pawns,
  threat tokens, markers, Central Core 02, mission cards, medals, and dice.
  Threat tokens replace bonus counters in Shadowraiders and are interchangeable
  with them in Total War.

## SH-SETUP-003 — Added board and pawn primitives

- **Source:** `sh-en-rulebook` — pp. 3–6, “New rules”.
- **Applies to:** Shadowraiders.
- **Maturity:** planned.
- **Rule:** Event spaces and drone-threat spaces introduce threats; Central Core
  02 uses a direction die. Pawns may expose a mission slot, hand-size or defense
  modifiers, Black ICE, mercenary cost, and stealth movement.
