Speedrunners or Zaibatsu Shadowraiders are required for play.

1. Number of players.
2. Approximate play time.
3. Which elements from Zaibatsu Speedrunners or Zaibatsu
Shadowraiders, or additional pieces (such as six-sided dice) are required
